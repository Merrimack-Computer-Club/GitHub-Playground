# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want everyone who uses this playground to have a respectful, harassment-free experience, whatever their major, background, or experience with Git.

## Our Standards

**Encouraged behavior:**
- Being kind and patient with learners at all skill levels
- Giving constructive, respectful feedback on Pull Requests
- Sharing what you know and helping other students when they are stuck

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Dismissive or demeaning comments about someone's skill level
- Deliberately messing with other people's practice work
- Any form of harassment or discrimination

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all interactions within this repository, including issues, pull request reviews, commit messages, and even contributions to the Markdown files.

## Reporting a Problem

If something here makes you uncomfortable, reach out to Peter Hajj on Discord or at hajjp@merrimack.edu or any other member of the Merrimack Computer Club eBoard. We'll talk with the people involved. Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in losing access to the playground or further disciplinary action by the club.
